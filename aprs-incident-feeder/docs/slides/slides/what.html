WHAT IT DOES

Incidents show up as APRS objects, and leave when they clear

APPEARS

A new incident becomes an object

A short name, a position, a symbol and one line of text.

CHANGES

An update moves or rewrites it

A tracked storm moves across the map as the warning is updated.

CLEARS

A kill packet takes it off your map

Nothing is left behind to linger after the incident is over.

The feeder checks its sources every two minutes and carries about 180 objects on a typical afternoon.

Open aprs.fi around Montgomery, Chester and Bucks counties and you see more than stations: a fire-truck symbol for a building fire, a crash symbol for an accident, a tornado symbol following a warned storm. The kill packet matters: APRS objects have no expiry of their own, so the feeder always retires what it created.
